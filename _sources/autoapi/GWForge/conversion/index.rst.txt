.. py:function:: time_to_coalescence(frequency, chirp_mass, symmetric_mass_ratio, mode=2)

   Time remaining to merger at a given frequency, to 3.5PN.

   This is the time-frequency relation that turns the frequency-domain response
   into a time-dependent one: the bin at frequency :math:`f` is emitted
   :math:`\tau(f)` seconds before merger, so the Earth's orientation there is
   the orientation at ``geocent_time - tau(f)``.

   Uses eq. (3.8b) of `arXiv:0907.0700 <https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0700>`_.

   :param frequency: Frequency in Hz.
   :type frequency: array_like
   :param chirp_mass: Detector-frame chirp mass in solar masses.
   :type chirp_mass: float
   :param symmetric_mass_ratio: :math:`\eta = m_1 m_2 / (m_1+m_2)^2`.
   :type symmetric_mass_ratio: float
   :param mode: Harmonic mode number. Mode ``m`` reaches a given frequency when the
                dominant quadrupole is at :math:`2f/m`, so ``tau_m(f) = tau_2(2f/m)``.
   :type mode: int

   :returns: Seconds before coalescence, positive and decreasing with frequency.
   :rtype: numpy.ndarray


.. py:function:: chirp_mass_and_symmetric_mass_ratio(parameters)

   Extract ``(chirp_mass, symmetric_mass_ratio)`` from a parameter dict.

   Accepts any of the mass parameterisations GWForge and bilby pass around:
   component masses, chirp mass with mass ratio, or chirp mass with symmetric
   mass ratio.

   :returns: ``None`` when the masses cannot be determined -- e.g. a custom non-CBC
             source model -- in which case the caller falls back to a static response.
   :rtype: tuple of float, or None
