.. py:function:: fit_model(model, samples, free_parameters=None, start=None, bounds=None, fixed_parameters=None)

   Fit a population model to samples by maximum likelihood.

   :param model:
   :type model: GWForge.population_fisher.base.PopulationModel
   :param samples: Event parameters to fit to. For a fiducial, these should be *all*
                   injections, not just the detected ones: the population model describes
                   the astrophysical population, and fitting to the detected subset would
                   absorb the selection function into the hyper-parameters.
   :type samples: dict
   :param free_parameters: Defaults to every hyper-parameter not in ``fixed_parameters``.
   :type free_parameters: sequence of str or None
   :param start: Starting point. Defaults to ``model.fiducial``.
   :type start: dict or None
   :param bounds: Name to ``(low, high)``. Merged onto :data:`DEFAULT_BOUNDS`.
   :type bounds: dict or None
   :param fixed_parameters:
   :type fixed_parameters: dict or None

   :returns: The fitted values, merged onto the starting point, with the keys
             ``"_success"``, ``"_message"`` and ``"_mean_log_prob"`` describing the
             optimisation.
   :rtype: dict
