   BGP primary mass and power-law mass ratio, with analytic scores.

   :ivar parameter_names: The eleven free hyper-parameters, using
                          :class:`GWForge.population.mass.Mass`'s spelling so that a generation
                          config and a Fisher config read the same.
   :vartype parameter_names: list of str
   :ivar mmin, m_high, maximum_mass: Support parameters, fixed by construction: hard cutoffs have a
                                     constant score, so their centred score vanishes and they are not
                                     measurable within Term I.

   :vartype mmin, m_high, maximum_mass: float
